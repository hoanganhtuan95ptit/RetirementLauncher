Retirement Launcher & Protection

Mô tả ngắn *
Simple, safe Android launcher for seniors with large icons and APK protection.

Mô tả đầy đủ *
Retirement Launcher & Protection is an easy-to-use Android launcher designed specifically for seniors, older adults, parents, and grandparents who want a minimalist, clean, and secure phone experience. The app transforms complex Android devices into a simplified home screen with large icons, intuitive navigation, and daily protection features.

Unlike standard launchers cluttered with overwhelming features, Retirement Launcher focuses on simplicity, accessibility, and protection. Everything is crafted to be easier to see, simpler to understand, and much safer for elderly users.

Whether you are setting up a phone for yourself or your loved ones, Retirement Launcher helps create a secure mobile environment, minimizing accidental taps and confusing operations.

🛡️ Protection Feature
